Liliia from the DAAD FK suggested to go on an easy hike near the mountains
given the weather was good. After the hike to [Auerspitz (1811 m)](/hikes/2024-10-12-auerspitz/)
I wanted something easier and gladly joined.

![View from Barmsee towards Klausenkopf (1181 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kruen/2024-10-20-00.jpg)

The trip started in Krün, where we got by bus from Kochel. The weather was just
right, making it a comfortable walk. By the time we got to Barmsee, we walked
past tons of other people of all ages walking by, many with dogs. Liliia didn't
take her corgi along, since the poor thing gets anxious in buses and trains
crowded with people.

![A hut near Barmsee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kruen/2024-10-20-01.jpg)

We then walked over to Geroldsee and to the village of Gerold itself. As the
path to the meadows opened, we saw a great view of [Zugspitze (2962 m)](/hikes/2025-09-18-zugspitze_hoellental/)
and the surrounding mountains. We had a picnic there, enjoying the view, then
started to loop back to the starting point.

![The forest trail between Geroldsee and Barmsee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kruen/2024-10-20-02.jpg)
![View from Geroldsee hills towards Zugspitze (2962 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kruen/2024-10-20-03.jpg)

On the way back we walked past a railway to Klais, but there were no buses any
time soon, so we headed into the forest to fast-track towards Krün. When we
reached Grubsee, we saw a bunch of parked rowboats and a paid entry to the
water. On the internet, it is [advertised as a swimming lake](https://www.alpenwelt-karwendel.de/baden-see-bayern-grubsee),
so I guess there's a well-kept beach during summer.

![A view towards Bletschertalkopf (1369 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kruen/2024-10-20-04.jpg)
![Cows in Gerold](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kruen/2024-10-20-05.jpg)

Finally, when we got most of the way through and walked past the Barmsee hotel,
we caught a bus that took us the rest of the way, then swapped to the bus back
in the direction of Kochel.

![Houses near the Kochel train station](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kruen/2024-10-20-06.jpg)

All in all, this is a good beginner hike for people of all ages, with views of
Mittenwald and Garmisch mountains. Definitely worth checking out outside of the
hot season.
