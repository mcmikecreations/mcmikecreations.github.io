This trip was done as the annual team-building exercise with the Chair for
Database Systems (and others). I was responsible for the organization, with
some strict rules to determine the route. It had to have a cable car, a
restaurant at the top, be not too long, have decent elevation gain, and be
accessible by train directly from Munich. The Wendelstein peak fulfilled
all these criteria, so we went there. We split off into three groups, one
starting at Geitau and going straight to the top, one starting at Geitau
and going across Türkenköpfl (1512 m), which I visited before going to
[Breitenstein (1622 m)](/projects/data-viz/hikes/breitenstein_wendelstein/) and
[Schweinsberg (1514 m)](/projects/data-viz/hikes/schweinsberg/), and the final
group starting in Osterhofen and taking the easier route up. The professors
went to Bayrischzell to have lunch and take the cable car up. I was in charge
of the group on the easier route.

![View from the asphalt road onto Bayrischzell](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wendelstein/2025-06-10-00.jpg)

The journey started safely along an asphalt road to Bergcafé Siglhof, with
us holding a very good pace up and making a single break at the cafe. We also
met a herd of alpacas on the way, which was cool, but one of them was overly
friendly to me and I got scared of getting bitten or spit on (I wasn't sure
if it was a llama or an alpaca, but it was definitely not a sheep).

![Alpacas](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wendelstein/2025-06-10-01.jpg)

After the break we continued our way up, this time on a gravel road, which led
us to Wendelsteiner Almen. By that time, we started making a bit more breaks, but
the pace was still good, and we were estimating to reach the top in time for our
reservation at the restaurant.

![View of Wendelstein from the Almen](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wendelstein/2025-06-10-02.jpg)

After the huts, the way up is a bit steeper and narrower, going on rocks and tree
roots, but it was still doable for all of us. We passed by quite a few hikers,
which was unusual for a Tuesday, but I guess mountains are popular on any day in
Bavaria. Some of us couldn't appreciate the view, though, taking calls instead.

![One of us taking a call on a scenic viewpoint](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wendelstein/2025-06-10-03.jpg)

When we were on the final rocky trail to the restaurant and the cable car station,
I got a message that the other groups have arrived and are having trouble with the
reservation, so I rushes up ahead of the group to figure out what was going on.
Turns out that the issue was solved quickly, and I stayed talking until the rest
of my group arrived. We also saw a bunch of paragliders and a glider flying by,
giving us something to look at despite the fog around the nearby peaks.

![A glider flying by Wendelsteinhaus](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wendelstein/2025-06-10-04.jpg)

After a quick lunch (18 Euro for Leberkäse and a Schorle by the way), we went to the
observatory at the top, making it just in time to see some football players posing
for a group photo. The last section to the peak was refreshing, with guard rails
and nice stairs all the way up. The view was not perfect due to the fog, but I was
still able to flex my knowledge of the local mountains.

![Football players posing for a group photo on Wendelstein peak](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wendelstein/2025-06-10-05.jpg)

![Group photo of our chairs](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wendelstein/2025-06-10-06.jpg)

We took a group photo on the peak too, going down shortly after. Some of us took
the cable car down, while the others split into two groups again, one following the
GPS track I prepared, and the other, including me, going down along Legerwald,
taking a break at Grüne Gumpe and going down to Freibad Bayrischzell. Although
we had quite a pace, we still arrived at the station later than the others.

![View from Zeller Scharte (1611 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wendelstein/2025-06-10-07.jpg)

Overall, it was a nice hike, which I managed to take from memory, without looking
at the GPS track or the digital map. I was also pleasantly surprised by my
physical condition, as I remembered the last time I went to Wendelstein, I had
more trouble with the ascent. As I proved on this outing, the route is doable
for beginners of completely different physical conditions, as long as you take
your time and make breaks. I highly recommend it for the views!
