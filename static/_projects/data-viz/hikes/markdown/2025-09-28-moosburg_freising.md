After the [Zugspitze Höllental hike](/404/) and a tough week, I was too tired to do a
difficult hike. As a solution, I opted for a more relaxed hike from Moosburg
to Freising. Took enough time to sleep in the morning, had nice breakfast,
and set off around 11 AM.

![Bridge over a local channel in Moosburg](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_freising/2025-09-28-00.jpg)
![Garten-Baumschule](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_freising/2025-09-28-01.jpg)

Moosburg itself looked quite plain, with people going about their daily lives.
The weather was okay, a bit cloudy but not too bad. I followed the signs
towards Niederhummel, passing by some fields and small farms. The trail was
well-marked and easy to follow.

![Common yarrow near the garden](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_freising/2025-09-28-02.jpg)
![Common chicory at the same spot](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_freising/2025-09-28-03.jpg)

Going through Thonstetten, I saw a white stork perched on a rooftop. In Ukraine
they are considered a sign of good luck, so I took it as a positive omen for
the hike.

![White stork at Thonstetten](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_freising/2025-09-28-04.jpg)

Near Oberhummel, I wanted to take a shortcut across a stream, but the wooden
bridge was missing. I had to backtrack a bit to find a proper crossing, that's
why the route looks weird.

![Fruit tree near Niederhummel](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_freising/2025-09-28-05.jpg)

On the way to Rudlfing along the river, I came across a small group of elderly
ladies trying to find a way to the Naturfreundehaus Freising Hangenham
restaurant. They asked for directions, and I suggested going back to Rudlfing
to make it there quicker. They politely declined, so I gave them directions to
Hangenham instead.

![Isar riverside](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_freising/2025-09-28-06.jpg)

I continued onwards to Freising, holding a pace of 6 km/h. At the station got
a nice Johannisbeer-Streuseltaler and headed home. There were tons of older
people on the route, so anyone is welcome to do a portion of it.
