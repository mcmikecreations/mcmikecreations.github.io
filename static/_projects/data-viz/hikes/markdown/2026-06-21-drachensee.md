After my hike to X, I needed some time to recover and take it easy. Although I
have a backlog of boring hikes for the ["Web"](/hikes/tag/Web/) category, I
found an invitation from Stanislav from my [Trainsjoch (1707 m) hike](/hikes/2026-01-18-trainsjoch/)
to visit Drachensee across the border from Garmisch. Without thinking much, I
accepted the invite.

![Starting the hike near the Ehrwald cable car](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-00.jpg)
![Approaching the gravel Hoher Gang via ferrata](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-01.jpg)

We started off quite steep, going through the forest towards the southern
cliffs and approaching the gravel slope of the via ferrata. We were by no means
early, because we were hoping for more hike participants, but the trail was
even more crowded than we expected. Some hiking groups even brought dogs,
although in retrospect I would not recommend that.

![Hiker cautiously approaching the Hoher Gang via ferrata start](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-02.jpg)
![View from the Hoher Gang via ferrata towards Zugspitze (2962 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-03.jpg)

We took a break on a bench with a nice view, then hydrated and set off.
The via ferrata itself wasn't particularly difficult or dangerous, I believe
it's difficulty A, but the gravel under your feet makes it a bit risky to get
hit with a rock on your way.

![Stanislav on the way up](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-04.jpg)
![The rocky Hoher Gang via ferrata route](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-05.jpg)

We got some scratches from the falling gravel, but nothing severe happened.
After finishing the climb, we walked onto the valley between the surrounding
ridges. The first thing we saw was Vorderer Tajakopf (2450 m) towering above.
I was told there's a climbing route there from Seebensee. Next, I noticed the
biking road going all the way to the lake, and a smaller hiking trail joining
it. We followed the trail and ended up at Seebensee.

![Rhododendron at the end of the Hoher Gang via ferrata](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-06.jpg)
![Vorderer Tajakopf (2450 m) from below](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-07.jpg)

The lake was very crowded, with many tourists swimming or just enjoying the
cool breeze coming off the water surface. There were tons of kids and dogs
running around, making the whole atmosphere even more cheerful. We stayed at
the lake for a bit, but then noticed the darker clouds and headed to our final
destination.

![View from Seebensee towards Drachenkopf (2303 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-08.jpg)
![Trail from Seebensee towards Drachensee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-09.jpg)

The ascent was longer than it looked. The trip took us roughly 30 more minutes,
and we were constantly fighting to stay on track while avoiding the hikers
going up and down, running, falling, or chasing each other. Some droplets
started falling from the sky, so we wanted to hurry up, which was impossible.

![The sign towards Drachensee near Seebensee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-10.jpg)
![The trail towards Drachensee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-11.jpg)

The path was rewarding though. Even before reaching the top, we saw Seebensee
with [Zugspitze (2962 m)](/hikes/2025-06-21-zugspitze_reintal/) in the background.
Some people were taking the detour towards the lake, taking their clothes off as
they went. Others put on rain jackets instead, covering themselves and their
backpacks from the miniscule amount of rainfall. We just kept going, trying to
get to the hut and grab some cold drinks.

![View from Drachensee towards Seebensee and Zugspitze (2962 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-12.jpg)
![Drachensee lakeside](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-13.jpg)

We reached the hut and ordered our drinks (6 Euro per drink, by the way). Then
headed outside to look at others trying to jump off cliffs into the lake or
crawling to the beach. The approach is quite steep, so I would have done the
same in their place.

![The way down from Drachensee towards Seebensee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-14.jpg)
![View towards Hinteres Tajatörl (2259 m) from below](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-15.jpg)

On the way down, we merged with a bigger group of Asian hikers. It felt a bit
uncomfortable walking alongside them, but I couldn't put my finger down on why.
After a while I realized, that on my hikes inside Germany slower hikers
typically let me through. This happened most of the time with German and
Austrian hikers on this route, but not with Asian ones. Culture differences, I
guess.

![Approaching Seebensee from above](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-16.jpg)
![Cow on the way](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-17.jpg)

As we walked past Seebensee and started our descent along the longer route
(Stanislav couldn't get down with his broken collarbone and elbow), we were
approached by a group of 3 cows. A pair of hikers approached the cows and tried
to pet them, but the cows got a bit aggressive towards them. I managed to keep
my distance, so I avoided their wrath.

![View towards Hinterer Igelskopf (2219 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-18.jpg)
![View towards the Ehrwalder Almbahn Bergstation](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-19.jpg)

The way down was quite nice in terms of scenery, but the sun was scorching. We
walked past Seeben Alm (1566 m) taking a toilet break inside, then headed down
around the cliff drop towards the upper cable car stations. We met more hikers
on the way refusing to let us through, although we did manage to overtake them
eventually on a wider section.

![Road from the Ehrwalder Almbahn Bergstation towards Ehrwald](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-20.jpg)

The rest of the way between the higher and lower cable car stations was easy,
just taking the gravel road (with some sections under repairs). As we reached
the bottom station with the bus stop, we had 40 more minutes of waiting, so
we went to a nearby restaurant to get some ice cream or beer.

![View from the road towards the cliffs and Drachenkopf (2303 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/drachensee/2026-06-21-21.jpg)

Overall, it's a must-visit hike for all ages. If you take pets, children, or
injured hikers, taking the longer way up might make more sense. I know it is
a very touristy and Instagrammy spot, but I promise it's enjoyable even for
the chronically offline people. Take enough drinking water with you though!

P.S. The whole reason I was overtaking people was to get back to Munich faster
and go watch the Backrooms movie funded by A24. Great movie, not just mindless
jumpscares.
