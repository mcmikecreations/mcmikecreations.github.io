I enrolled in a driving school to finally get a driving license I've been
meaning to get since I turned 18 6 years ago. First, I was busy grinding for
grades and learning German to enroll at TUM, then I was busy grinding for
grades at TUM to get into the PhD. Finally, I realized I can't delay it any
longer and started visiting the course.

![Lake Ammersee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/herrsching_tutzing/2025-02-23-00.jpg)

After a rough week in a stuffy office room listening to the theoretical course
in German for 3 hours a day, I decided to take a stroll along an easy route to
clear my mind. Out of the option I had, e.g. Murnau to Kochel, Penzberg to
Tutzing, Brannenburg to Prien, [Munich to Starnberg](/404/)... I decided to pick
Herrsching to [Tutzing](/404/). The goal being, of course, to connect my hiking route
endpoints into a single net, since I'm walking anyway and might as well do
something cool with it.

![Kurparkschlößchen Herrsching](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/herrsching_tutzing/2025-02-23-01.jpg)

Since I'd need to change the train in Laim anyway, I decided to pick the
direction of the hike on the spot based on which train came first.

![Look at Kloster Andechs](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/herrsching_tutzing/2025-02-23-02.jpg)

The trains decided to stop at Passing, making me take several busses to get
there, but eventually I ended up in Herrsching. I wandered around and took
the longer trail to [Kloster Andechs](/404/), then took the official X7 Wanderweg to
Tutzing through Machtlfing and Traubing. It was already raining at 15:15 when I
started, so there were not a lot of other hikers on the route.

![Look from Kloster Andechs onto the fields](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/herrsching_tutzing/2025-02-23-03.jpg)
![Kloster Andechs itself](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/herrsching_tutzing/2025-02-23-04.jpg)

What struck me immediately was the deer farms and the dairy production
facilities plastered everywhere around Herrsching. At the lake I came across
many foreigners. I thought to myself, that there are more English-speaking
people here than in downtown München.

![Local jam vending machine](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/herrsching_tutzing/2025-02-23-05.jpg)

Regardless, I continued walking and soon I was alone on the trail, going
through fields and small forests. After 10 kilometers I started coming across
horse riders on evening walks and woodchucks at work (on a Sunday?), which
gave me some hope I'm not that far from civilization. Around Traubing it
finally got dark and I decided to take a 2-minute break to have a protein bar
and some rest after doing 5-6 kmh for the entire route. Although I got wet
sitting on the bench under the rain near a random field, the break was very
much needed and deserved.

![Deer farm on the way to Machtlfing](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/herrsching_tutzing/2025-02-23-06.jpg)

Another thing that struck me was the thickness of the surrounding forests. When
hiking in the mountains, I'm used to seeing sparse spruce forests that you can
traverse easily. But here, with mixed trees and other plants of all kinds, the
forests created a deep shade like an umbrella over the surrounding area. Even
during the day it felt like I was walking at dusk.

![Child mosaic in Tutzing](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/herrsching_tutzing/2025-02-23-07.jpg)

Regardless, I pressed on, pointing out the lack of lion statues in the gardens
of the expensive villas in the countryside, until I arrived at Tutzing and
headed home. By then the railway was repaired and I could change in Laim
without hopping on busses or trams.

One of my original plans was to scout the area out for potential organized
group hikes in the future. As a result, I was left wondering if it's worth it.
The route is 20 km, the scenery is not so breathtaking, and most of the way is
just an asphalt road. Nothing to write home about. Still, compared to mountain
hikes, maybe someone will enjoy the flatness of this route.
