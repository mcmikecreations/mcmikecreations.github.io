After my last trip to [Roßkopf (1579 m)](/hikes/2024-09-21-rosskopf/), I was excited to explore the
other ridges in the Spitzingsee area. Since I've been to [Aiplspitz (1759 m)](/hikes/2024-08-31-aiplspitz/),
which is part of the greater mountain ridge near Raukopf (1691 m), taking on
more peaks from this ridge seemed like the logical next step. Rotwand (1884 m)
was the highest, so I went with it, inspired by [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/germany/bavaria/spitzingsee-taubenstein-rotwandhaus).

![Spitzingsee with a view of Bodenschneid (1669 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rotwand/2024-09-29-00.jpg)
![Wet spruce spikes on the way](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rotwand/2024-09-29-03.jpg)

The hike started off nice with good weather and a tiny layer of snow on the
hillside. Immediately after leaving the bus, I was greeted with a view of
[Bodenschneid (1669 m)](/hikes/2025-11-01-bodenschneid/) across the lake. I
rushed uphill overtaking the other tourists, trying to reach the cooler
scenery.

![View from Schönfeldhütte (1410 m) towards Jägerkamp (1746 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rotwand/2024-09-29-01.jpg)
![View from Schönfeldhütte (1410 m) towards Berggaststätte Panoramablick (1613 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rotwand/2024-09-29-02.jpg)

By the time I reached Schönfeldhütte (1410 m) and saw the view of
Jägerkamp (1746 m) that I [visited in the past](/hikes/2024-08-31-aiplspitz/), the ground turned all
wet and muddy. With the hikers constantly taking the same trails, the mud spots
were getting bigger by the minute. I looked up and saw
Berggaststätte Panoramablick (1613 m), the cable car restaurant that most of
the elderly hikers were targeting.

![View towards Tanzeck (1703 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rotwand/2024-09-29-04.jpg)
![View towards Rotwandhaus (1737 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rotwand/2024-09-29-05.jpg)

I skipped Taubenstein (1693 m) because I already started to feel tired and
wanted to at least get to my destination. It was nice seeing all the families
enjoying the nature, covered with more and more snow as I kept going. After
passing the Kirchstein saddle, I saw Rotwandhaus (1737 m) in the distance.
That's when I knew my destination was right around the corner!

![View from Rotwand (1884 m) towards Großtiefental-Alm (1500 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rotwand/2024-09-29-06.jpg)
![View from Rotwand (1884 m) towards Auerspitz (1811 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rotwand/2024-09-29-07.jpg)

The last few bits of the road were easy, with a tiny climb up the rock that
marks the peak of Rotwand (1884 m). The view was amazing, with the snow-covered
hills and the surrounding summits in full glory. I saw Auerspitz (1811 m) to
the south-east, which I [plan to visit soon](/hikes/2024-10-12-auerspitz/).
To the north-east, I saw Hochmiesing (1883 m) and the
Großtiefental-Alm (1500 m) hut. There was also Dürrmiesing (1863 m), but there
seems to be no official trail towards it.

![View from Rotwand (1884 m) towards Taubenstein (1693 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rotwand/2024-09-29-08.jpg)

I then went back down to Rotwandhaus (1737 m) and saw a panorama of the
surrounding valley with Hinteres Sonnwendjoch (1986 m) in the distance. Those
mountains look amazing, although I don't have a good idea of how to get there.
Well, this is a problem for future me to tackle! For now, getting scared of the
restaurant prices, I skipped the meal inside and headed to Spitzingsee. The
rest of the trip was uneventful. I just followed the wide dirt road used to
deliver food to the hut, then took the bus and train to Munich.

![View from Rotwandhaus (1737 m) towards Hinteres Sonnwendjoch (1986 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rotwand/2024-09-29-09.jpg)

I'd say this is a very good route for medium-level hikers. It is slightly
longer and higher than what many tourists are used to, but the well-kept path
and the views definitely make up for it. And since it's the highest peak on the
ridge, the sense of accomplishment is also great!
