I had Krottenkopf (2086 m) on my list of future hikes for quite some time,
ever since my hike to [Aiplspitz (1759 m)](/404/). Scrolling through the list
on Thursday evening, I
stopped at [Pyramidenspitze (1997 m)](/hikes/2025-02-02-pyramidenspitze/)
and Vordere Kesselschneid (2002 m), [Soiernspitze (2253 m)](/hikes/2025-01-19-soiernspitze/) over
Signalkopf (1895 m), and Krottenkopf (2086 m) via Bischof (2033 m). The latter
caught my eye the most, considering the previous visit to neighboring
[Hoher Fricken (1940 m)](/hikes/2025-03-30-hoher_fricken/) in March this year.

![Farchant road](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-00.jpg)

Despite the forecast of possible rain in the afternoon, I decided to go for it,
and posted an invite in some of the hiking chats I frequent. Bohdan, a member
of a Ukrainian hiking group in Munich, responded positively, and we agreed to
take an early train to Garmisch-Partenkirchen on Sunday morning.

![View down to Farchant](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-01.jpg)
![Hunter's hut on the way up](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-02.jpg)

After exiting in Farchant, we set off towards Frickenkar, passing over a few
dried-out mountain streams. The trail wasn't well-marked at times, but it was
impossible to get lost, as there was only one main path leading up. The
ascent was steady but not too steep, allowing us to gain decent altitude.
I wasn't able to move up as fast as Bohdan, who is used to hiking longer
distances and higher elevations, so I was the one setting the pace. We even got
to see some mountain goats grazing on the slopes below us!

![Frickenkar reached](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-03.jpg)
![Mountain goats](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-04.jpg)
![View from Frickenkar plateau onto Hoher Fricken (1940 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-05.jpg)

After reaching Niederer Fricken (1682 m), we turned north-east and continued
towards the Bischof (2033 m) peak along Oberauer Steig. I took the shorter
way up to the summit, going on top of some rocks, while Bohdan wanted to make
a swift ascent along the longer trail that he skipped last time he was here.
It was nice seeing [Wank (1780 m)](/404/) from this mountain, bringing back
memories from a past adventure.

![Trail up Bischof (2033 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-06.jpg)
![Henneneck, Kareck (2046 m), Oberer Rißkopf (2049 m), and Krottenkopf (2086 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-07.jpg)
![Hoher Fricken (1940 m) and Wank (1780 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-08.jpg)

After reaching the peak, we took a few photos and quickly headed down to hide
from the incoming wind and clouds. The weather was still holding up, but we
knew that rain could start at any moment. We continued our descent towards
Krottenkopf (2086 m), following the ridge line.

![Bischof (2033 m) peak](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-09.jpg)

Around this point I started getting cramps in my legs due to the lack of sleep
the night before and food in the morning. This slowed us down quite a bit, as
I had to take frequent breaks to stretch and hydrate. Bohdan was very
understanding and patient, waiting for me whenever I needed to stop.

![Weilheimer Hütte (1946 m) and Krottenkopf (2086 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-10.jpg)

Reaching Weilheimer Hütte (1946 m), we met a few other hikers standing there.
Bohdan headed straight to the top of the mountain, trying to beat his ascend
record from last time. I stayed and chatted with the others for a bit. Turns
out, all the huts in the area were already closed for the season, so we
couldn't get any warm drinks to fight the cold wind.

![The reverse view from the Krottenkopf (2086 m) slope down to Weilheimer Hütte (1946 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-11.jpg)

After saying goodbye to the others, I started my ascent and got to the top
shortly after. The route to the summit of Krottenkopf (2086 m) was much easier
and not nearly as steep as the way up to Bischof (2033 m). The wind was
also not as strong, allowing me to enjoy the view for a bit longer.

![Krottenkopf (2086 m) peak](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-12.jpg)
![Trail down from Weilheimer Hütte (1946 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-13.jpg)

On the way down, I recorded my descent with an action camera, although nothing
breathtaking happened. I'm certain the Krottenkopf (2086 m) section of the
route is possible even in winter after some fresh snowfall. The path is
well-trodden and not too steep, with only 130 m of elevation gain from the hut.

![View westwards to Archtalkopf (1927 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-14.jpg)
![View eastwards to Hohe Kisten (1922 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-15.jpg)

We continued our way to Eschenlohe, passing by other peaks I left for another
time, such as Oberer Rißkopf (2049 m), Schindlerskopf (1940 m), and
Archtalkopf (1927 m).

![Spruce near Archtalkopf (1927 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-16.jpg)
![Path down Pustertal Kar](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-17.jpg)

After descending another rocky section, we reached
Pustertal Jagdhütte, and after those the way down turned into a simple forest
trail.

![Pustertal Jagdhütte](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-18.jpg)
![Eschenlohe horses](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-22.jpg)

There is a horse ranch at the end of the route, where I stopped for a few quick
photos, and headed to the train station in Eschenlohe. Of course, everything
in the town was closed on Sunday, so we had to wait for the train at the
station and board it without any snacks or drinks.

![Local child art in Eschenlohe](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-19.jpg)
![Bridge across Loisach](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-20.jpg)
![View from the Loisach bridge](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/krottenkopf_bischof/2025-11-16-21.jpg)

To sum up, this version of the hike is great for those looking for a bit more
of a challenge compared to the standard route. For everyone else, I would
recommend taking the shorter and easier path directly to Krottenkopf (2086 m),
since the views from the summits are quite similar. Also, consider going in
summer during the opening hours of Weilheimer Hütte (1946 m) to get some 
food and drinks there. Best of luck!
