With an extra holiday at hand, I decided to visit some interesting spots near
Moosburg an der Isar. Although I didn't plan the route too carefully, I think I
made it work at the end. Continuing the [Hiking Web](/hikes/tag/Web/)
project, since I already visited [Moosburg an der Isar](/hikes/2025-09-28-moosburg_freising/),
it was the logical next step to follow Isar all the way to Landshut.

![Restaurant Avlija Brzovic](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-00.jpg)
![Wayside shrine in the field](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-01.jpg)

My first checkpoint was Berghofen, a small village on a local hill. On the way
to it, I crossed some fields with deer, ducks, and other animals enjoying the
sun.

![Wild deer on the way](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-02.jpg)
![One of the local lakes near the quarry](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-03.jpg)

As I got closer, I also came across a few local lakes where it was forbidden to
swim, and a local quarry. Every time I would approach the lake beach, a few
wild ducks would get startled and flee the area, shouting at me to stay back.

![The quarry near Bühlhäusl](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-04.jpg)
![Horses on the way to Berghofen](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-05.jpg)

On the top of the Kuhberg (487 m) hill, I first visited the 14th century church
of St. Peter und Paul, then enjoyed the view towards Moosburg from a viewing
area near the church.

![St. Peter und Paul 14th century church](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-06.jpg)
![View onto the Steinbuckel (474 m) hill](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-07.jpg)

Afterward, I made a quick loop around the village, making sure to pass by the
top of the hill. There were a bunch of families enjoying the holiday dinner
together, with their kids running around in gardens and on the streets.

![Schloss Kronwinkl](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-08.jpg)
![A field near Zacherlmühle](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-09.jpg)

My next stop was Kronwinkl, with Schloss Kronwinkl as the target. I saw it from
far away and was thrilled to see an actual castle in quite good condition. As
usual, the inside of the castle was private, with actual people living in
different sections split into apartments.

![A field near Ast](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-10.jpg)

Although my initial plan was to go to Viecht and stay on the beach trails near
Isar, I decided to go to Ast instead, specifically to Schloss Ast. I ignored
the chore of calculating the route and distance to that village, and just
followed the signs and common sense to get there. Surprisingly, I even met some
women having walks with baby carriages far away from the surrounding villages.

![St. Georg church in Ast](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-11.jpg)

When I got to the castle, there was a gate saying that entry is forbidden. I
had to go over a field and climb into a garden near the castle to get inside,
finding out there's a normal open entrance on the other side. Still, the
castle itself was closed, so I just kept going.

![The blooming Tiefenbach](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-12.jpg)
![The bull statue at Metzgerei Tremmel](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-13.jpg)

I saw some ruins in Schlöttholz near Tiefenbach on the map, so I set it as my
next target. The way there was really nice, since there was a bike road the
whole way through. Walking through the village was uneventful, but the forest
section at the end was quite cool.

![An old weight limit sign for trucks and tanks](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-14.jpg)
![Liverleaf near the medieval settlement](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-15.jpg)

The ruins themselves were just a dirt wall that once circled a medieval
settlement. I started following some random trail hoping to see something cool,
but it didn't really lead anywhere and just looped in a circle.

![The hill on which the medieval settlement is located](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-16.jpg)
![White sharp-lobed liverleaf](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-17.jpg)

Then, I went to Burgstall Schlossberg near a Naturfreude cafe on top of a hill.
There were a bunch of people enjoying refreshing drinks, but I was in a hurry
and didn't have company of my own, so I skipped it and kept going. In any case,
I knew this was the last stretch of the hike, as I was already approaching
Landshut.

![The view near Burgstall Schlossberg](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-18.jpg)
![Radio tower at Achdorf](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-19.jpg)

First, I went to Birkenberg (473 m) in Achdorf. It was right near a radio tower
covered in graffiti, making for a cool location to check out. There was also a
lot of machinery nearby drilling a water well.

![The view from Birkenberg (473 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-20.jpg)

Second, I planned to go straight to the castle but saw a hill peak icon of
Annaberg (463 m) in a random neighborhood. I couldn't just walk by and headed
there to check it out. It was, as expected, a quiet upper class neighborhood,
so I didn't dwindle there for too long.

![Burg Traunsnitz from below](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-21.jpg)
![Burg Traunsnitz up close](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-22.jpg)

Third, I headed to Burg Traunsnitz to check out the Landshut panorama. There
were tons of locals and tourists there, and even a tour guide giving a
historical lesson on the construction and happenings of the castle. I took some
photos and headed to the museum cafe, since at that point I already ran out of
all food and water I was carrying.

![My dinner at Burg Traunsnitz](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-23.jpg)

It was a bit weird drinking my mango juice and eating some cake while the other
visitors were taking long drinks and cocktails. There was also a group of
russians arguing with each other while ordering more and more alcoholic drinks.
The wife was threatening the husband with "knocking even more teeth out of his
mouth than before", while the husband was telling her to shut her mouth and
take nice pictures of him near the sunset. I ignored this typical russian
family as much as I could and tried to appreciate the panoramic view instead.

![The panoramic view from Burg Traunsnitz](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-24.jpg)

But with all things considered, and with the russian group starting to get
violent between each other, I decided to head out to the train station. I
quickly ran down the stairs to Alte Bergstraße and entered the old town.

![The panoramic view from Burg Traunsnitz](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-24.jpg)
![St. Martin's Church in Landshut](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-25.jpg)

Walking across the old town of Landshut was really cool. At first, I was just
looking at the old buildings and the locals taking evening walks, but having
ice cream shops on every corner got the best of me, and I bought a
pomegranate-pistachio cone.

![Crossing Pfettrach on the way to the train station](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/moosburg_landshut/2026-04-03-26.jpg)

The rest of the way was uneventful. I'd say this was a nice hike, but I can't
really recommend it, as there's not much to see apart from the castles. Maybe,
it would be a good bike ride instead?
