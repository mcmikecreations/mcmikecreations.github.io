There were a lot of medium-sized mountains in the Tegernsee area that I didn't
visit, unlike [Gindelalmschneid (1335 m)](/404/) and [Wallberg (1722 m)](/404/).
Under the premise of going mountain carting to Spitzingsee, I took David,
who I've been hiking with since [Rampoldplatte (1422 m)](/hikes/2025-02-09-rampoldplatte/),
out for a hike to some of the missing peaks.

![Bergwacht car parking near the mountains](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wasserspitz/2025-09-07-00.jpg)

We set off near Kalkofen and started heading up. The road was wide and not
steep at all, suitable for quads, bikes, and pretty much anything that moves.
We passed by quite a few groups of hikers and bikers along the way.

![Trail near Kühzagl-Alm](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wasserspitz/2025-09-07-01.jpg)

Starting the hike with a light walk up past Kühzagl-Alm and around
Brunnerstein (1243 m), we enjoyed the warm summer weather and discussed our
recent updates.

![View from Raineralm onto Rinnerspitz (1611 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wasserspitz/2025-09-07-02.jpg)

After reaching Raineralm, we started the "real" hike by ascending the trail to
Wasserspitz (1552 m). The first section was shallow and wide, with kettle
around us. Once we came up to the mountain ridge, coming across some senior
hikers going down, the trail became steeper. I caught myself thinking that
going up this trail in winter or after rain would be uncomfortable.

![View from the Wasserspitz (1552 m) ridge](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wasserspitz/2025-09-07-03.jpg)
![Route towards Wasserspitz (1552 m) summit](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wasserspitz/2025-09-07-04.jpg)

Getting to the summit was quite straightforward. We kept going up along a
narrow path on the ridge.
After reaching the Wasserspitz (1552 m) peak we took a short lunch break, where
I enjoyed my cheesecake stick and coffee that I brought along. Somehow, after
a few hikes with such a meal, this became my go-to for medium difficulty hikes.

![View from Wasserspitz (1552 m) towards Eckspitz (1386 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wasserspitz/2025-09-07-05.jpg)
![View from Wasserspitz (1552 m) towards Rinnerspitz (1611 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wasserspitz/2025-09-07-06.jpg)

Once we were ready, we continued along the ridge further in the direction of
Rinnerspitz (1611 m). At first, everything was going okay, although some parts
were narrower and steeper than I would have preferred. Then, we started
noticing more and more ants along the route. A bit later, the ants were
everywhere! On the path, on every rock and tree, on our shoes, our hands, in
our socks even! We had to speed through the section to the Georg Jennerwein
memorial just to get rid of them.

![Herb-robert aka Geranium robertianum](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wasserspitz/2025-09-07-07.jpg)

When we got there and assessed the trail ahead, we saw that it was quite steep
and muddy. We (unfortunately) didn't set a goal to visit every peak on this
hike, so we chose to head down to the Bodenschneidhaus (1365 m) and follow the
road from there.

![Dried cowslip aka Primula veris](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wasserspitz/2025-09-07-08.jpg)
![Rowan tree aka Sorbus aucuparia near Bodenschneidhaus (1365 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wasserspitz/2025-09-07-09.jpg)

The way down to the hut was quite steep and went across gravel, so it was quite
easy to slip. Trying to keep my balance, I got stung by nettle, which afterward
nagged me for another day or two.

![Cows near Obere Firstalm (1369 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wasserspitz/2025-09-07-10.jpg)

We made another break in the shade of the boulders near the hut, with David
climbing one of them. After that, we kept going along Sommerweg down to
Obere Firstalm (1369 m). Not wanting to make any more breaks, we immediately
headed further down the slope to Untere Firstalm and up to Suttenstein.

![View from Stümpfling (1506 m) onto Bodenschneid (1669 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wasserspitz/2025-09-07-11.jpg)
![View from Jagahütt'n onto Spitzingsee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wasserspitz/2025-09-07-12.jpg)

Finally reaching Jagahütt'n, we rented the mountain carts, waited for 40
minutes in the line, and finally drove down.

![Ready to take mountain carts down](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/wasserspitz/2025-09-07-13.jpg)

If anyone is interested in a similar trip with a mountain cart ride down, I
would suggest selecting any of the neighboring mountains and planning the route
to finish in Jagahütt'n. The peaks we went to on this day weren't the highest
or most picturesque ones, so you're good regardless of which ones you pick for
your adventure. Have fun!
