To recover from the [Hoher Kalmberg (1833 m)](/hikes/2026-03-06-hoher_kalmberg)
hike, I and Pavlo decided to take it easy and do a shorter hike. After a lot of
negotiating, I lowered the difficulty level further, and we chose to hike up
the Geißwand (872 m) trail.

![Stairs leading up from the train station](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/geisswand/2026-03-07-00.jpg)
![The forest section of the hike](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/geisswand/2026-03-07-01.jpg)

We took the train to Traunkirchen Ort and started our hike from there. The
trail was well-marked and not too steep, making it a nice and relaxing walk.
The first section to the bottom of the rocky hill goes through a forest, with
moss and flowers covering the ground and the trees. Some small parts were
nicely covered in stairs, making the climb even easier.

![A Hepatica flower along the way](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/geisswand/2026-03-07-02.jpg)
![A hand-made cross on top of a slope](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/geisswand/2026-03-07-03.jpg)

After the forest trail, the real ascent started. Although we still walked
through the forest, the trail under our feet changed from nice packed dirt into
stones, loose rocks, dry leaves, tree roots, and mud. Considering our winter
shoes were wet from the day before, we took our spare city sneakers for this
outing, making slipping a very real threat. Still, we continued up, enjoying
the warm weather and meeting tons of other hikers on the route.

![The view from Baalstein (800 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/geisswand/2026-03-07-04.jpg)
![Pavlo taking the visitor log book to read at Baalstein (800 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/geisswand/2026-03-07-05.jpg)

When we reached the plateau near the peak, I convinced Pavlo to take a detour
to Baalstein (800 m). There are 3 or 4 trails to reach it, some involving
climbing, some not. We took the path with a tiny rope-supported climbing
section to make it more fun, leaving our backpacks at the base of the rock. I
took a stick at the train station to help me with the climb, forgetting to
bring it back after the climb. The view from the top was nice, but the trees
covered most of the views.

![Rope climbing wall on the way to Geißwand (872 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/geisswand/2026-03-07-06.jpg)
![Pavlo climbing to the top of Geißwand (872 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/geisswand/2026-03-07-07.jpg)

After going back to the base of the rock, we continued to the Geißwand (872 m)
peak. The trail turned into a chain-supported climbing wall, with some
overhangs and a few steeper sections. It was fun and a first experience for
Pavlo, making it a memorable moment for us.

![The view from Geißwand (872 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/geisswand/2026-03-07-08.jpg)
![Cliff visitors enjoying the view, pre-smoking](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/geisswand/2026-03-07-09.jpg)

The view from there was much better, with a clear view of lake Traunsee
and the higher mountains in the background. We stayed there for a bit, taking
some photos and taking in the sun. Suddenly, someone brought out an electronic
cigarette and started vaping, which was an immediate mood killer. We were
already uncomfortable with the cigarette and nicotine pouch vending machines
everywhere in Austria, even in small villages. This experience just made it
worse, and we quickly left the peak to get away from the vape smoke.

![View from Geißwand (872 m) towards Traunkirchner Kogel (1575 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/geisswand/2026-03-07-10.jpg)
![View deeper into the mountains](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/geisswand/2026-03-07-11.jpg)

On the way down, we noticed that most trains skip our initial train station,
so we had to take a different path to a bigger station. We approached the
lakeside and followed the road to the train station, where we took the train
to Gmunden.

![Pavlo crossing a cliff drop on the way down](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/geisswand/2026-03-07-12.jpg)
![Lakeside view of the mountains](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/geisswand/2026-03-07-16.jpg)

We had a really nice time on this hike, enjoying the views and the warmth.
It is very accessible to people of all ages and skill levels, with many
children, dogs, and elderly hikers on the trail. Make sure to get shoes with
a good profile to not slip on the route!
